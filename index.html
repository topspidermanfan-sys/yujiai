Choose your plan

Switching plans here is instant and free — it's a demo toggle, not a real charge. See the note below for what it takes to make it real.

Heads up: this page can't process real payments on its own — it's a static front end with no server. Switching plans just changes a stored value in your account so you can see how the limits behave. To actually charge people, you'd add: a payment processor (Stripe or Paddle) to collect cards, a small backend to create checkout sessions and receive webhook events, and a database that flips the user's plan only after the processor confirms payment — never on the click alone.
